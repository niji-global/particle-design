Philosophy

To make an idea real, an abstract concept
has to be turned into a concrete structure.

Concept, business, operations, data and technology are different layers. We connect them, and put them to work in society as one continuous experience.

Problem

The hard-to-see gap
between strategy and implementation.

Why do so many good ideas and strategies stall once it is time to carry them out? Because a step is missing: taking an idea that is still vague, building it into day-to-day operations, and connecting the actual work with data and systems.

As long as those who draw up the strategy and those who build the systems are kept apart, an idea will not truly take hold in society.

What We Do

As a business co-creation studio,
we turn ideas into new value that can be put into practice.

We are neither simply a consulting firm nor simply a systems developer. We take the time to understand the business you have in mind, and work alongside you from end to end: designing the business, setting up how the work gets done on the ground, and building it with technology.

Approach

Sharpen the picture,
then take a sure step.

01 — Discover

Find the problem

What is the real problem? What value should the business truly offer? Through thorough dialogue and research, we set the compass for the project.

02 — Design

Design the structure

Moving back and forth between the business model, workflows and system requirements, we design a foundation that can be carried out and extended.

03 — Implement

Give it form

With prototypes, apps and digital touchpoints, we test and improve, again and again, until the idea takes solid form.

04 — Operate & Scale

Grow and extend

Going live is just the beginning. We continue to help operations settle in on the ground, make improvements based on data, and scale the business.

Founder

Representative Director and CEO

Tomohito Wajima

Business Architect / Strategic PMO / Implementation Partner

He has worked in business development from end to end, from concept to commercialisation, company formation and execution, in insurance, insurtech, healthcare, blockchain, traceability and new business development.

After product development and new business development at AIG, the AXA Group and other companies, he moved forward projects at the SBI Group including an AI cancer genome project, the formation of a joint venture with a European unicorn company, and the founding and commercialisation of SBI Traceability Co., Ltd.

Today, as head of Particle design Inc., he helps companies and regions with value they have yet to put to work: analysing it and making the most of it through business, operations, data and technology, and turning it into a form that society can use.

Proof of Execution

Why you can leave it to us, through to implementation.

Drawing up a strategy is not enough to set a business in motion. Particle design connects ideas to business structure, workflow design,
data and project organisation, and brings the practical know-how to move them forward to implementation.

01

Turning ideas into business structure

We break an abstract idea down into customer value, revenue structure, operations and project organisation, and shape it into the framework of a business that can be carried out.

02

Moving cross-departmental projects forward

Standing between management, business units, the front line, development and outside partners, we sort out the issues and set up a project organisation that keeps decisions and execution moving.

03

Connecting operations and technology

Starting from an understanding of how the work is actually done, we bridge it to system requirements, data design and operating processes, and implement it as something people use.

04

Looking beyond implementation to operation

Going live is not the end goal. With adoption, improvement cycles, use of data and scale in view, we design for something that keeps running.

05

Standing between the business side and the development side

We translate the intentions of the business side and the constraints of the development side, and put requirements, priorities and the implementation process in order so that both can head for the same goal.
