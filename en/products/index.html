    CASK / BARREL

    Cask management for whisky distilleries and wineries

    CASK / BARREL is a cask management service developed and provided by Particle design Inc. One ledger for every cask: where it is, what is in it and what was measured, shared by the handheld app in the warehouse and the portal in the office, and linked to the blender’s tasting and trial-blend records.
